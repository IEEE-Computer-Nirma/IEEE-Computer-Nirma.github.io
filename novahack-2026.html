Track 1 · LegalTech & Agentic AI PS-01

Autonomous Contract Review Agent

Problem Statement

Legal teams and businesses spend significant time manually reviewing contracts to identify potential risks, ensure compliance with internal policies, and verify the presence of critical clauses. This process is highly dependent on expert review, making it slow, expensive, and difficult to scale.

Contracts such as NDAs, employment agreements, and vendor agreements often contain ambiguous language, unfavorable obligations, missing terms, or deviations from company standards that may expose organizations to legal and financial risks.

Organizations need a more efficient way to analyze large volumes of contracts, identify potential issues early, maintain consistency with internal policies, and assist legal professionals in making faster, more informed decisions.

Key Challenges

  • Manual contract review requires significant lawyer hours.
  • Important clauses or risks may be overlooked due to human limitations.
  • Comparing contracts against company policies is repetitive and time-consuming.
  • Historical contract knowledge is difficult to reuse effectively.
  • Organizations lack a centralized understanding of recurring legal risks and patterns.
Track 2 · Enterprise & Workflow AI PS-02

AI Meeting Intelligence Platform

Problem Statement

Organizations conduct thousands of meetings where important decisions, action items, and business context are discussed, but much of this information is lost after the meeting ends. Existing meeting tools primarily focus on transcription and summarization rather than converting conversations into actionable workflows.

Teams often struggle to track decisions, assign ownership, update project management systems, communicate outcomes to absent stakeholders, and identify blockers emerging from discussions.

Organizations need a system that can understand meeting context, extract meaningful business outcomes, and seamlessly connect conversations with existing workflows to improve execution and accountability.

Key Challenges

  • Meeting information is scattered across recordings, notes, and chat platforms.
  • Decisions and action items are often forgotten or poorly tracked.
  • Project management tools require manual updates after discussions.
  • Teams lack visibility into blockers and dependencies.
  • Stakeholders who miss meetings struggle to stay aligned.
