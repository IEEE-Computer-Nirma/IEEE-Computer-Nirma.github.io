About

The IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter at Nirma University was established to inspire and mentor young engineers passionate about computing, cybersecurity, and emerging technology.

We organize hands-on workshops, technical competitions, research programs, and networking events throughout the academic year, giving students a platform to build real skills beyond the classroom.

Our flagship event, NovaHack 2026 (August 22–23, 2026), features a 24-hour hackathon and the NovaHack CTF, a Capture The Flag arena which is kept open in general after the event for continuous practice.

Chapter Values

  • — Learn by doing — theory matters less than building
  • — No prerequisites — curiosity is enough
  • — Open source and community-first mindset
  • — Research and innovation beyond coursework
